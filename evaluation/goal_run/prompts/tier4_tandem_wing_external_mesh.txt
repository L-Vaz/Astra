Do an incompressible 3D flow over a tandem wing configuration. The mesh is
provided as a .msh file with boundaries 'inlet', 'outlet', 'walls', 'airfoil',
'frontAndBack'. inlet/outlet are freestream at 9 m/s; walls/airfoil are no-slip;
frontAndBack are type wall. Run t=0 to 10, deltaT=1.0, output every 1.
nu = 1.5e-05 m^2/s. Use simpleFoam with the SpalartAllmaras turbulence model.
Visualize velocity magnitude along the mid-Z section at the final time.
