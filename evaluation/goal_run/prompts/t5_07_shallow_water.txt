Perform a shallow-water simulation of flow in a flat rectangular basin using the
shallowWaterFoam solver. The basin is 10 m x 10 m in the x-y plane, meshed with blockMesh as a
structured 50x50x1 grid one cell thick in z. The left boundary named "inlet" has an inflow
velocity of 0.5 m/s in +x. The right boundary named "outlet" is an open outflow with zero
gradient. The top and bottom boundaries named "sideWalls" are slip walls. The front and back
faces named "frontAndBack" are empty. The initial water depth h is 0.1 m everywhere, at rest,
over a flat bed (h0 = 0). Gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s^2. The simulation runs from
time 0 to 20 seconds with a time step of 0.1 seconds, and results are written every 5 seconds.
