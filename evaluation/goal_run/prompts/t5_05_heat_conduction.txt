Perform a 2D transient heat conduction simulation in a solid square plate using the
laplacianFoam solver. The plate is 1.0 m x 1.0 m with a thickness of 0.01 m in z. Use blockMesh
with a structured 50x50x1 mesh. The left boundary named "hotWall" has a fixed temperature of
373 K. The right boundary named "coldWall" has a fixed temperature of 273 K. The top and bottom
boundaries named "topWall" and "bottomWall" are adiabatic (zero gradient). The front and back
faces named "frontAndBack" are empty for 2D. The initial internal temperature is 273 K. The
thermal diffusivity DT is constant at 4e-5 m^2/s. The simulation runs from time 0 to 2000
seconds with a time step of 10 seconds, and results are written every 500 seconds.
